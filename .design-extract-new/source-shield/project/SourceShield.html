Three quiet guarantees

A shield you never have to think about.

01

Sealed at the source

Raw transcripts live only inside encrypted memory. We never log them, never store them in the open, never let them touch the dashboard.

02

Sanitized on arrival

Names, dates, numbers and places soften into safe tokens before a journalist reads a word — context preserved, identity withheld.

03

A thread that stays open

Pseudonymous two-way follow-up keeps the conversation alive over iMessage — without ever revealing who is on the other end.

What we won't pretend

Honesty is part of the protection.

The shield is real, and so are its edges. We tell sources exactly where it ends.

The boundary

Pseudonymous, not anonymous.

Carriers, Apple and the messaging platform still see phone metadata. We protect the contents of a tip — never the existence of a phone.

The signal

A similar claim is a lead, not proof.

When separate channels echo the same story, we surface it as a signal worth chasing — never as independent corroboration.
